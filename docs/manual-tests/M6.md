# M6 To-do, island, customization: manual checklist

Covers FR-TODO-01 to 05, FR-ISL-01 to 12, FR-CUS-01 to 03.

| # | Step | Expected | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Command mode: "add call Mom to my to-do list". | "call Mom" appears in To-do > Today. | [ ] |
| 2 | Command mode: "add gym at 6 PM" (before 18:00). | Item due today at 18:00. After 18:00 the same phrase gives tomorrow 18:00. | [ ] |
| 3 | Wait for 18:00 (or set a due time 2 minutes ahead). | A notification with Done and Snooze 10 min appears. Snooze brings it back 10 minutes later; Done ticks the item. | [ ] |
| 4 | To-do page: add, edit, tick, delete, and drag to reorder. Switch Today / Upcoming / Done. | All work and the order sticks after reopening the window. | [ ] |
| 5 | Hover over the island pill for half a second. | It expands to a panel with Music, Plans, Clipboard, Apps, Wallpapers, Quick. It collapses about 0.8 s after the pointer leaves. | [ ] |
| 6 | Scroll down on the pill, then keep scrolling on the panel. | The panel opens, then scrolling cycles through all six tabs. | [ ] |
| 7 | While idle, click just beside the pill. | The click goes to the window underneath (only the pill is clickable). | [ ] |
| 8 | Try to focus the island with Alt+Tab; check the taskbar. | It never takes focus and has no taskbar button. | [ ] |
| 9 | Music tab while Spotify plays. | Title, artist, artwork, progress and working play/pause, next, previous. Changes update without reopening. | [ ] |
| 10 | Plans tab. | Today's to-dos with checkboxes and a count like 0/3, and the next 3 routines. | [ ] |
| 11 | Clipboard tab: turn it on, copy three texts, click one while a text box has focus. | The clicked item is pasted. Quit and restart: the list is empty. | [ ] |
| 12 | Settings > General: pin apps; open the Apps tab. | Up to 12 pinned apps; click launches. | [ ] |
| 13 | Settings > General: set a wallpaper folder; open the Wallpapers tab. | Thumbnails appear; click sets the wallpaper. | [ ] |
| 14 | Quick tab: pause hotkeys, choose a microphone, mute, autostart, switch Soul / Loadout / Skin. | Each applies immediately. | [ ] |
| 15 | Play a video full screen (F11 in a browser). | The idle island hides; it shows again when you use a hotkey. | [ ] |
| 16 | Windows Settings > Accessibility > Visual effects > Animation effects off. | Island and window animations stop. | [ ] |
| 17 | Customize > Skin: switch Midnight, Paper, Ember. | The island and main window change at once, no restart. | [ ] |
| 18 | Duplicate a Soul preset, rename it, add an addendum, Save, Use this preset; give a command. | The reply follows the new tone. Dictation is unchanged. | [ ] |
| 19 | Export a preset, delete it, Import the file. | It comes back. Editing `schema_version` to 9 and importing shows a clear error. | [ ] |
| 20 | Check the default Loadout on a fresh install. | Full. | [ ] |
| 21 | Hover the pill, move away, click it, trigger a dictation. | Each change morphs smoothly (springy size and corners, content fades in); nothing jumps. | [ ] |
| 22 | Settings > General > TaskHub: sign in with your TaskHub account. | "Signed in as …". To-do page says "Synced with TaskHub" and shows your TaskHub to-dos (personal ones and business tasks assigned to you). | [ ] |
| 23 | Command mode: "add buy milk at 7 PM". | It appears in TaskHub (web app) for today 19:00, and in Wisp. | [ ] |
| 24 | Tick a to-do in Wisp; add one in the TaskHub web app. | The tick shows in TaskHub; the new one shows in Wisp within about 2 minutes. | [ ] |
| 25 | Sign out. | Wisp's own local list comes back. | [ ] |
