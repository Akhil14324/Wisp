# M3 Circle to Ask: manual checklist

Covers FR-CTA-01 to 09. Needs a second monitor at 150% scaling for steps 4-5.

| # | Step | Expected | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hold Ctrl+Shift on the primary monitor. | After ~120 ms the screen dims 20% with a crosshair cursor and a hint at the top. | [ ] |
| 2 | While holding, drag a circle around a word and ask "what does this mean?", release. | The overlay closes; a card appears next to the circle with a Markdown answer about that word. | [ ] |
| 3 | Draw two separate strokes in one hold. | Both strokes show with a glow, and the answer considers both areas. | [ ] |
| 4 | Move the mouse to a second monitor at 150% scaling. Circle a table cell and ask "what is the total?". | The answer refers to the circled content. | [ ] |
| 5 | Check the crop: circle a small area and ask "describe exactly what is in the close-up image". | The description matches the circled area (within about 24 px). | [ ] |
| 6 | Hold Ctrl+Shift and ask a question without drawing. | The answer uses the whole screen. | [ ] |
| 7 | Hold Ctrl+Shift, draw, but do not speak (or under 300 ms). | The card opens with a text box; type a question and press Enter to get an answer. | [ ] |
| 8 | On an answer, click Follow-up and ask something else; repeat until 5 turns. | Follow-ups use the same images; after 5 turns Wisp says to start a new circle. | [ ] |
| 9 | Hold Ctrl+Shift with the mouse over the open card and speak a follow-up. | It is answered in the same card. | [ ] |
| 10 | Click Copy, then paste somewhere. | The answer text is pasted. | [ ] |
| 11 | Press Esc on the card. | The card closes. | [ ] |
| 12 | Ask "is the dim overlay or a red circle visible in the first image?" | No: the overlay, island and card are excluded from capture (only the red strokes drawn by Wisp appear). | [ ] |
| 13 | Several input languages installed: press Ctrl+Shift. | If the layout switches, Settings > Hotkeys shows the note and link to fix it. | [ ] |
